# Sales Performance Dashboard

## Business Problem

The sales team wants to understand revenue trends, top-performing products, customer segments, and regional performance to improve business decisions.

## Tools

- Excel
- SQL
- Power BI

## Dataset

500 Records (Generated)

## Business Questions

- Which region generates the highest sales?
- Which products have the highest profit?
- Which month performs best?
- Which customer segment contributes most revenue?

## Dashboard Features

- KPI Cards
- Monthly Sales Trend
- Region-wise Sales
- Product Analysis
- Customer Segment Analysis

## Business Insights

- West region contributes 38% of total sales.
- Office Supplies have the highest sales volume.
- Technology products generate the highest profit margin.

## Recommendations

- Increase inventory for top-selling products.
- Focus marketing on high-performing regions.
- Improve underperforming product categories.
